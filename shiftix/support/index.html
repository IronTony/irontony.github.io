Shiftix Support

For help or feedback, open a support request on GitHub. A GitHub account is required. Requests are public, so do not include personal or sensitive information.

Include your device model, operating system version, app version, and what happened. Avoid sending sensitive personal information.

How to play

Slide rows and columns to sort gems into nine matching blocks. The middle row and column of each 3×3 block are locked.

Choose your experience

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard on Home. Themes offers Jelly, Classic, Candy and Blocky Animals. Settings lets you choose your language and adjust sound, vibration, ambient effects and move hints.

Saved games

Use Save during a game to name your puzzle. Open Saved games on Home to resume it. The delete button removes an individual save. Saves are stored on your device.
