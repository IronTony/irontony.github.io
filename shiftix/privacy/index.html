Shiftix Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026.

This policy describes how Shiftix handles information. For questions, use the support page.

Information in the game

Shiftix stores game saves and preferences locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not send your saves or preferences to developer-operated servers. The game does not include advertising, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking services.

Device storage and deletion

Saved puzzles remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete individual saved games from Saved games. Device backups and restores may retain app data according to your operating system and backup settings.

Support requests

Support requests submitted through GitHub Issues include your GitHub username and the information you choose to provide. Requests and replies are public and are used to respond to your issue. Do not include personal or sensitive information. Requests remain on GitHub until deleted, subject to GitHub’s policies and retention practices. You can edit your submissions and request removal through the support page.

Other services

Apple and Google handle app distribution and may process information under their own privacy policies. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement. The website has no advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms.

Children

The game does not request personal information from players. Please avoid including children’s personal information in support requests.

Policy changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
